The Service

Two Ways We
Protect Your Fabric

Guarding and pre-shrinking are two separate treatments that do two different jobs — one protects the surface from stains, the other keeps the fit and shape true over time. We offer both, and recommend asking about them together on any new order.

Fabric Guarding

A protective treatment applied to upholstery fabric, helping it resist spills, stains and everyday wear. Applied to new custom pieces or existing furniture, it's a simple way to keep your investment looking its best for years to come.

Stain & spill resistance

New or existing upholstery

Safe on all fabric types

Extends fabric life

Fabric Pre-Shrinking

A treatment applied to fabric before it's cut and fitted, so your finished piece keeps its shape over time. It's a small step that stops later shrinkage from pulling seams and corners out of line.

Prevents shrinkage after fitting

Keeps seams and corners sharp

Recommended for natural fibres

Applied before cutting begins
