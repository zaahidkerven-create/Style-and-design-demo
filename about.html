About Style & Design

A Family-Run
Atelier, Est. 1988

Established in 1988 as Master Interiors CC, Style and Design (Pty) Ltd is now a leading manufacturer and exporter of high quality bespoke furniture, appreciated throughout the African continent. To this day, we maintain our reputation for excellent craftsmanship and outstanding service to clientele ranging from interior designers to high-end homeowners, including the corporate and hospitality industries.

We specialise in all types of upholstery furniture, and each piece is tailored to suit our client's unique specifications. Our factory is based in Johannesburg and also provides contract manufacturing and outsourcing services across Southern Africa.

— Style & Design, Johannesburg
