The Service

Given New
Life

A tired frame doesn't mean the end of a favourite piece. Our reupholstery service strips a sofa, chair or headboard back to its bones, repairs what needs repairing, and rebuilds it in fabric of your choice — keeping the piece you love, made to last another lifetime.

What's Included

Every Reupholstery Job

Frame Inspection & Repair

Every frame is checked for damage and reinforced where needed before any fabric goes on.

Foam & Padding Renewal

Old, flattened padding is replaced with fresh foam and fibre for lasting comfort.

Spring Retensioning

Sagging seats are re-webbed and re-sprung so the shape holds for years to come.

Fabric Fitting & Finishing

Hand-cut and fitted in your chosen fabric, finished with the same care as new furniture.

Tailored
To Fit

Loose and fitted covers are a lighter-touch alternative to full reupholstery — tailored precisely to your existing or newly built furniture, giving you a fresh look and everyday protection without recovering the piece itself. It's one of the things we're best known for, and a large part of what we make every week.
