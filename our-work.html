OUR WORK

Real Projects, From Family Homes To Luxury Lodges

A Legacy In Every Stitch

Pieces We've Actually Built

Since 1988, every piece to leave our workshop has been shaped by hand — family sofas reupholstered with care, loose covers cut to fit, cane seats rewoven strand by strand, and suites built new for lodges across Southern Africa. What follows is a glimpse of that work, gathered from decades of clients who trusted us with the furniture they love most.
